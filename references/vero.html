Vero is a luxury fine-art studio that transforms your wedding dress into a timeless sculpture.

Each Vero sculpture stands approximately 15 inches tall and weigh 5–10 pounds. Our sculptures are made of plaster, ceramic, and resin and are crafted with exceptional precision and detail, exactly as it was worn on your wedding day.
