# 3D models

- `letov-guitar.glb` — "Guitar Musima 1655 HV Egor Letov" by Kirill_lewis
  (sketchfab.com/klintonf81), Sketchfab Standard licence.
- `tuners.glb` — the six tuners, with the strings wound on their posts, cut
  out of "Ovation Guitar" by Duane's Mind
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ovation-guitar-f5bd8065de084cf297302ed676f4da71),
  licensed under CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Changed: only the tuners and windings kept, rescaled and set on the Letov
  model's posts, recoloured silver. Built by `scripts/build-tuners.mjs` from
  the downloaded `scripts/models/ovation-guitar.glb` (not in the repository).
